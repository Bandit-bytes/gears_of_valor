## Armor sets

Wear all four pieces of a set to get its bonus.

| Set | Material | Full set bonus |
|---|---|---|
| **Woodland Spirit** | Tainted Iron | Speed and Jump Boost, invisible while sneaking, Resistance on grass, dirt, leaves or logs. **Thornroot:** melee attackers are rooted and pricked. |
| **Arisma's Awakening** | Arisma's Scale | Regenerates while standing still, slows nearby hostile mobs. Enchanted weapons deal +5% damage per enchantment (max +25%). |
| **Paladin** | Noble Gem | Resistance and Fire Resistance, heals nearby allies, burns nearby undead and takes 25% less damage from them. **Last Stand** below 30% health. |
| **Reaper's Embrace** | Soulinite | Weakens nearby hostile mobs, lifesteal on melee hits, Strength II and Speed below 40% health. **Death's Door:** survive a killing blow once every 5 minutes. |

## Weapons

Every weapon has a passive effect and a right-click ability.

| Weapon | Passive | Right-click ability |
|---|---|---|
| **Harbringer** | **Omen:** hits mark the target; marked targets take +20% damage. | **Herald's Toll:** shockwave that damages, repels and marks nearby foes. |
| **Fury's Call** | **Rising Fury:** chained hits build up to 5 stacks (+6% damage each); the 5th hit ignites the target. | **Battle Cry:** Strength and Speed, weakens nearby foes. |
| **Heaven's Fall** | **Judgment:** +40% damage against the undead. | **Heaven's Wrath:** holy lightning on the creature you're looking at. |
| **Shadow Stalker** | **Soul Reap:** hits wither the target; killing blows restore 2 hearts. | **Shadow Step:** teleport behind the creature you're looking at. |

## Materials

- **Fallen** and **Soulinite** ores generate deep underground (below Y 30). Smelt or blast the raw ore into ingots.
- **Unseen Soul** drops from the Wither.
- **Purification Stone** is sold by max-level Cleric villagers.
- **Noble Gem ritual:** drop a Cursed Moonshard and a Purification Stone into a water cauldron heated from below by fire, soul fire, a lit campfire, lava or a magma block.

## Requirements

- [Architectury API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/architectury-api)
- [AzureLib](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/azurelib)
- [Fabric API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fabric-api) (Fabric only)

Works with [Better Combat](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/better-combat-by-daedelus) (weapon attack animations) and [3D Skin Layers](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/skin-layers-3d).

## Building

```sh
./gradlew build
```

The jars are written to `fabric/build/libs/` and `neoforge/build/libs/` (`forge/build/libs/` on the 1.20 branch).
